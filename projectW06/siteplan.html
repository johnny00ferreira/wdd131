Project Site Plan

Site Name

Clean Beaches

The name Clean Beaches was chosen because it is clear, direct, and easy to understand, immediately communicating the main purpose of the website: to promote awareness about recycling, waste reduction, and beach preservation. The name is easy to remember and strongly connected to environmental responsibility, aligning with the educational and informational goals of the project.

Site Purpose

The purpose of the Clean Beaches website is to educate and inform users about the importance of recycling and keeping beaches clean. The site provides practical tips on waste reduction, proper recycling practices, and ways individuals and communities can actively participate in beach preservation initiatives. It also encourages social responsibility and environmental awareness through clear and accessible content.

Scenarios

  • How can I properly recycle waste collected from the beach?
  • What actions can I take to help keep beaches clean in my community?
  • How can I participate in beach clean-up initiatives?

Color Scheme

  • Ocean Blue (#1E90FF): Used for headers, navigation elements, and highlights.
  • Sand Beige (#F5DEB3): Used for background areas and section containers.
  • Dark Gray (#333333): Used for body text to ensure readability.

Typography

  • Arial: Used for body text due to its readability and simplicity.
  • Montserrat: Used for headings to provide a modern and clean appearance.
